About Movie:

  "Maharshi" is a Telugu-language film directed by vamshi paidipally,starring Mahesh Babu,Pooja Hedge,and Allari Naresh.The film revoles around a successful bussinessman who revisits his past to reconnect with his roots and help the people who were instrumental in his journey to success.It explores themes of friend ship social responsibility ,and the impact of ones action on the society.The movie received positive reviews for its storyline,performences,and emotional depth.
